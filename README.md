## 👋 About us

**Worldxgrowth** is a software development agency from India. We design, build and launch complete products for startups and businesses — from blockchain platforms and crypto payment systems to business software, websites and mobile apps.

- 🚀 **End-to-end delivery** — planning, UI/UX, development, deployment and support
- 🔐 **Web3 specialists** — wallets, exchanges, DEX, bridges, tokens, staking and payment gateways
- 📱 **One team, every platform** — web, Android, iOS and Windows desktop
- 🤝 **Long-term partner** — we stay with you after launch for updates and growth
